devBertini AdMob

A private tool for the developer's own AdMob account.

devBertini AdMob is a small program the developer of the devBertini apps and games runs on his own computer. It uses Google's AdMob API to read his AdMob account (the apps, the ad units and the earnings reports) and, only when he asks for it, to change that account's apps and ad units.

It is not offered to anyone else: there is no sign-up, and the only Google account it signs in with is the developer's own.

How it handles Google user data is described in the Privacy Policy; its use is covered by the Terms of Service.
